Behind the Blooms

From Fresh Petals to Timeless Keepsakes

WrapUpBlooms began with a simple belief: that the beauty of a flower shouldn't wilt in a week, and that the gifts we carry every day should hold deep personal meaning.

Our Heritage

Crafting Tangible Reminders of Love & Friendship

When we first launched WrapUpBlooms on Etsy, we noticed that while people love fresh floral bouquets, what they truly yearn for is something permanent — a pocket mirror that lights up during an intimate wedding rehearsal, a leather lipstick sleeve clipped to an everyday handbag, or an engraved wooden family puzzle resting on the living room mantle.

By merging botanical birth month illustrations with permanent precision laser engraving, we turned everyday functional accessories into cherished keepsakes. Over 12,400 customers later, our mission remains unchanged: handcrafting every piece with patience, care, and heartfelt devotion.

Behind the Scenes

Our 5-Step Artisan Process

From your custom order to her hands, here is how we ensure each gift is flawless.

01

Botanical Drafting & Personalization Proof

Your recipient's name and selected birth month flower are matched against our curated vector typography to ensure harmonious proportions.

02

Precision Laser Etching

Executed by our trusted New Hampshire production partner with microscopic laser passes that etch cleanly into leather or metallic compact surfaces.

03

Hand Cleaning & Mirror Calibration

Every mirror and casing is manually inspected for optical clarity, LED halo battery charge, and seam alignment.

04

Boutique Ribbon Gift Boxing

Nestled into our signature pink and cream presentation box, hand-tied with double-faced satin ribbon and an embossed botanical care card.

05

Fast Localized US Dispatch

Carefully packaged in protective mailers and dispatched in 1-2 business days with full door-to-door tracking.
